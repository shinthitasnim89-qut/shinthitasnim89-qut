## About Me

I am an **MPhil researcher in Information Systems at Queensland University of
Technology (QUT)**. My research explores how machine learning and digital twins
can represent evolving information and support informed decisions.

My experience spans **healthcare AI, wearable disease prediction, assistive

in Australia and Bangladesh.

## Current Research

### Personalised Digital Twins for Emergency Stroke Decision Support

I am developing a persistent, patient-specific digital twin to represent
clinical information throughout an emergency stroke episode.

My work focuses on:

- **Patient-state modelling:** representing patient context and sequential
  clinical observations.
- **Temporal reasoning:** distinguishing clinical change from delayed or
  corrected evidence.
- **Traceability:** maintaining observation provenance and interpretable
  state histories.
- **Evaluation:** assessing the value of evolving patient representations
  for diagnostic support.

Implementation work includes **MIMIC-IV/ED data processing**, machine-learning
models and research prototypes using **FastAPI and Streamlit**.
